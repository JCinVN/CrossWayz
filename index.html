How to Play ✕

Scoring (Letter → Word → Turn)

Tap Letter presets to build a Word into the Subtotal — each tap appears as its own tile below the number so you can always see exactly what you've tapped. Tap W for a Wild card (worth 0). Press − or + to commit that Word into "This Turn's Points". Repeat for each Word you play this turn. Nothing is final until you tap Submit Round Score.

Bonus (2X / 3X / TAX / FLAT)

After building your Words, pick a Bonus and where it applies: Letter (choose a card, self-explanatory value), Word (auto-picks your highest word for a multiplier, or lowest word for TAX), or Turn (applies to the whole turn total so far). Multipliers add the extra copies; TAX subtracts that amount; FLAT just adds a flat amount unrelated to any Letter/Word/Turn value. One Bonus per turn.

Perfect Turn (+50)

A flat bonus you can trigger once per turn, stacking on top of any 2X/3X/TAX/FLAT you also used. It also triggers automatically the moment a single Word is built from exactly 7 cards (the whole hand at once) — no need to press the button yourself in that case. Double-tap the greyed-out button to undo either way.

Lookup (−5)

A quick penalty for the current turn. Once per turn — double-tap the greyed-out button to undo it before you submit.

Challenge (+10 / −10)

Pick who's being Challenged and who's the Challenger — they don't have to be the current player. Whoever wins gets +10, whoever loses gets −10, applied immediately. Once per turn; double-tap either button to undo.

Card Exchange

Max. 3 cards / 1 per round / 3 per game. Using it scores 0 for your turn and skips straight to the next player. A brief pop-up lets you undo it right after. Any Challenge points earned this round are kept separately and aren't affected.

Handicap

Only shown for players who had it checked at setup. 1 per round / 3 per game — a usage counter with an undo pop-up, same as Exchange.

🤝 Team Work Round

A full Round played together, triggered by the current player (the Leader). Everyone plays a shared pool of points: the Leader gets 120%, players who played a card get 100%, players who didn't get 50% (capped at 25). Since the whole Round was consumed by it, play jumps straight back to the Leader on the next Round number — nobody's seat is skipped and nobody plays twice.

 Wager Session

A standalone side-activity — NOT a Round or a Turn. Anyone can trigger it any time; the game simply pauses, resolves the Session, then continues exactly where it left off (same player, same Round). The Scratchpad calculator inside is just scratch paper and never affects anyone's score — only Buy (−20) and HC Lookup (−10) actually deduct points. Each Session is logged independently, so triggering it more than once in the same Round shows up as separate rows in Round Details.

♠ T-PAIR Time!

A distinct purple alert (separate from normal toasts) that reminds the scorekeeper a T-PAIR reward applies. Fires automatically for: a Challenge being decided, a Perfect Turn (manual or auto), a Long Word (auto-detected from the Score → Letter Count mapping in Setup), the 3rd Word committed in a single Turn (Intersections), a Word scored ≥15 using a Wild card (Wild 15), and a completed Team Work Round.

Game Timer & Turn Timer

Game Timer is an optional whole-game countdown set at setup, flashing the last 30 seconds with a beep alarm at zero. Turn Timer is per-player and starts only when you tap ▶ Start Turn — it ticks in the last 10 seconds and gongs at zero. Both share the same pause button.

Ending the Game

Tap End Game twice to confirm. On the Final Settlement screen, pick who went out first (+25 bonus) and enter each player's Hand Points (a built-in calculator is available). Confirm to see the ranked Final Results.
